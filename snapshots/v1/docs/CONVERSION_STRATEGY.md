# FR Conservas - Conversion Strategy

## Conversion Objective

The primary conversion objective is to generate WhatsApp conversations that lead to product inquiries and sales.

The page should optimize for one main action:

"Consultar por WhatsApp"

This action should be available in the hero, product area, final CTA, footer, and potentially as a floating mobile button.

## Conversion Model

The user journey should be:

1. Understand the offer.
2. See product quality.
3. Trust the preparation and brand.
4. Confirm the available products.
5. Contact by WhatsApp.

The conversion strategy is not based on pressure. It is based on clarity, appetite, trust, and convenience.

## Primary CTA Strategy

Recommended CTA label:

- "Consultar por WhatsApp"

Alternative CTA labels:

- "Hacer un pedido"
- "Consultar disponibilidad"
- "Pedir informacion"

Best default:

"Consultar por WhatsApp"

Reasoning: it reduces commitment pressure and fits both consumers and commercial buyers. A user can ask before buying, which lowers friction.

## WhatsApp Message Strategy

The WhatsApp link should include a prefilled message to reduce typing effort.

Recommended message:

"Hola FR Conservas, quiero consultar por productos disponibles."

For product-specific CTAs:

"Hola FR Conservas, quiero consultar por [producto]."

For commercial inquiries:

"Hola FR Conservas, quiero consultar por pedidos para comercio."

## CTA Placement

### Hero

Must include the primary WhatsApp CTA above the fold on mobile.

Purpose:

- Immediate action for users who already want to buy.
- Clear commercial direction.

### Product Cards

Each product can include a compact CTA:

- "Consultar este producto"

Purpose:

- Convert product-specific interest into WhatsApp conversation.

### Final CTA

Should appear after product and trust content.

Purpose:

- Capture users who needed more context before acting.

### Footer

Should include WhatsApp, Instagram, and email.

Purpose:

- Provide backup contact options without competing with the main CTA.

### Floating WhatsApp Button

Recommended on mobile and desktop, but it must not obscure content.

Purpose:

- Keep the primary conversion path always available.

## Conversion Friction Points

Potential friction:

- User does not know what products are available.
- User does not know if products are for personal or commercial purchase.
- User cannot assess quality from visuals.
- User is not ready to place an order and wants to ask first.
- User needs trust before contacting.
- Missing contact details or unclear CTA wording.

Recommended solutions:

- Use "consultar" language rather than only "comprar".
- Show products clearly with images.
- Mention both personal and commercial inquiries.
- Explain the care behind the product.
- Use a short FAQ.
- Keep the WhatsApp CTA consistent.

## Trust Strategy

Trust must be earned through design, photography, copy, and structure.

Trust builders:

- Real product photography.
- Logo visible and consistent.
- Premium but warm color palette.
- Clear ingredient/process claims.
- Direct contact methods.
- Professional layout with no clutter.
- Accessibility and readability.

Avoid false trust builders:

- Fake testimonials.
- Unsupported awards.
- Generic stock photography.
- Overly polished corporate language.

## Product Clarity Strategy

The product section should be concrete and easy to scan.

Each product card should include:

- Image.
- Product name.
- One short description.
- Product-specific WhatsApp CTA.

The cards should avoid long text. The goal is recognition and appetite, not a full product sheet.

## Motivation Strategy

The page should speak to practical use cases:

- Picadas.
- Meals.
- Shared tables.
- Gourmet shelves.
- Commercial resale.

This makes the product easier to imagine in real life and increases purchase intent.

## Audience-Specific Conversion Paths

### Consumers

Need:

- Product appeal.
- Simple ordering path.
- Confidence in taste and quality.

Best CTA:

"Consultar disponibilidad"

### Commercial Buyers

Need:

- Professional presentation.
- Supplier confidence.
- Direct contact.

Best CTA:

"Consultar para comercio"

Recommended approach:

Use the main CTA for WhatsApp, then include a secondary text line in the final CTA or FAQ:

"Tambien recibimos consultas para comercios y revendedores."

## CRO Recommendations

- Keep the first viewport focused on product, brand, and WhatsApp.
- Avoid a large nav that competes with the CTA on mobile.
- Use one primary button style consistently.
- Repeat the CTA after meaningful decision points.
- Use product-specific WhatsApp messages.
- Do not add carousels; they hide content and increase interaction cost.
- Do not add decorative sections that delay the product view.
- Use a FAQ only if it removes real purchase doubts.
- Make product photos large enough to inspect on mobile.
- Keep final CTA visually distinct but not visually loud.

## Conversion Checklist

- Primary CTA appears above the fold on mobile.
- WhatsApp CTA text is clear and repeated consistently.
- Product cards include direct action.
- Commercial buyers are acknowledged.
- No section distracts from product clarity or contact.
- CTA contrast meets accessibility standards.
- Floating button does not cover footer links or content.
- Contact information is visible at the end of the page.

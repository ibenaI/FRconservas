# FR Conservas - Content Strategy

## Content Role

The content must make FR Conservas feel artisanal, premium, close, and trustworthy. It should explain the product clearly, create appetite, and guide users toward WhatsApp without sounding aggressive.

The page should answer three questions quickly:

1. What does FR Conservas sell?
2. Why should I trust the product?
3. How do I buy or ask for availability?

## Content Principles

- Write in simple, human Spanish.
- Prioritize clarity over ornamental copy.
- Use short paragraphs and scannable sections.
- Mention concrete product details whenever possible.
- Avoid generic luxury language that feels inflated.
- Avoid technical, corporate, childish, or aggressive tones.
- Never invent testimonials, certifications, stock availability, or production claims.

## Brand Voice

Recommended tone:

- Cercano.
- Claro.
- Confiable.
- Gastronomico.
- Cuidado.
- Profesional.

The voice should feel like a premium local food brand, not a mass supermarket product and not a cold corporation.

Example direction:

- "Conservas artesanales elaboradas con ingredientes seleccionados."
- "Sabores simples, cuidados y listos para compartir."
- "Consultanos por disponibilidad o pedidos para comercios."

Avoid:

- "Los mejores del mercado" unless backed by proof.
- "Oferta imperdible" or urgent pressure.
- Excessive adjectives like "unico", "increible", "inigualable" without specifics.

## Content Hierarchy

### Level 1: Immediate Understanding

The hero should communicate:

- Brand: FR Conservas.
- Category: conservas / encurtidos artesanales.
- Products: berenjenas and morrones.
- Action: consult or order by WhatsApp.

Suggested H1 options:

- "FR Conservas"
- "Conservas artesanales FR"
- "Encurtidos artesanales para disfrutar y compartir"

For SEO and clarity, the strongest option is likely:

"Conservas artesanales FR"

It gives the brand and category in one line.

### Level 2: Product Confidence

The next content should reinforce:

- Ingredients selected with care.
- Artisanal preparation.
- Premium presentation.
- Useful for meals, picadas, and commercial resale.

### Level 3: Product Details

Each product needs concise, useful copy:

- Product name.
- Flavor/usage description.
- Contact CTA.

Recommended product copy:

**Berenjenas encurtidas**

"Berenjenas preparadas con un proceso cuidado, pensadas para sumar sabor a picadas, platos y mesas compartidas."

**Morrones encurtidos**

"Morrones encurtidos de sabor intenso y presentacion artesanal, ideales para acompanar comidas o sumar a una propuesta gourmet."

### Level 4: Trust and Process

The About section should explain enough to reduce doubt:

- Small-scale or artisanal production if accurate.
- Ingredient selection.
- Care in preparation and packaging.
- Direct contact with the brand.

Suggested heading:

"Elaboracion artesanal, presentacion cuidada"

### Level 5: Conversion

The final CTA should be concise:

"Consultanos por disponibilidad y pedidos"

Supporting copy:

"Escribinos por WhatsApp para conocer productos disponibles, precios y opciones para comercios."

## Information Architecture

Recommended section order:

1. Hero: offer and CTA.
2. Benefits: why the product is worth attention.
3. About: process and trust.
4. Products: clear catalog.
5. FAQ: remove common doubts.
6. Final CTA: WhatsApp.
7. Footer: contact alternatives.

This order supports the user's natural decision path: understand, trust, inspect, ask.

## SEO Strategy

### Search Intent

The page should target users looking for artisanal preserves, pickled products, gourmet food, and local food suppliers.

Likely search intent:

- Buy artisanal preserves.
- Find pickled eggplants or pickled peppers.
- Discover gourmet products for picadas.
- Contact a supplier for stores or resellers.

### Primary Keyword Themes

- conservas artesanales
- encurtidos artesanales
- berenjenas encurtidas
- morrones encurtidos
- conservas gourmet
- productos artesanales

### Local / Commercial Keyword Themes

If the business serves a specific city or region, the future landing page should include that location in metadata, body copy, and structured content.

Potential themes:

- conservas artesanales en [ciudad]
- encurtidos artesanales en [ciudad]
- proveedores de conservas artesanales
- conservas para comercios

The current source files do not specify location, so location-based SEO should not be invented yet.

### Recommended Metadata

SEO title:

"FR Conservas | Conservas y encurtidos artesanales"

Meta description:

"Conservas artesanales FR: berenjenas y morrones encurtidos con ingredientes seleccionados, elaboracion cuidada y contacto directo por WhatsApp."

Open Graph title:

"FR Conservas - Encurtidos artesanales"

Open Graph description:

"Berenjenas y morrones encurtidos con sabor artesanal, presentacion cuidada y contacto directo para pedidos."

### Heading Strategy

Use one H1:

- Conservas artesanales FR

Use H2 for major sections:

- Productos artesanales para tu mesa
- Elaboracion cuidada
- Nuestros productos
- Preguntas frecuentes
- Hace tu consulta por WhatsApp

Use H3 for products and FAQ questions.

### Image Alt Strategy

Alt text should describe the product or brand asset, not stuff keywords.

Examples:

- "Logo de FR Conservas con ilustracion de frasco artesanal"
- "Frasco de berenjenas encurtidas FR Conservas"
- "Frasco de morrones encurtidos FR Conservas"

## Content Gaps To Resolve Before Final Landing

The current source of truth does not include:

- WhatsApp number.
- Email address.
- Instagram URL or handle.
- Business location or service area.
- Product photos.
- Exact jar sizes or presentations.
- Pricing or availability.
- Wholesale conditions.
- Testimonials or reviews.

These gaps should be resolved before writing final production copy. If not available, the landing can still launch with generic contact copy, but it should avoid claims that need proof.

## Content Quality Checklist

- The offer is understandable in less than 5 seconds.
- WhatsApp action is visible without scrolling on mobile.
- Product names are explicit.
- Copy explains quality through specifics, not empty adjectives.
- No unsupported social proof is included.
- Each section has one clear purpose.
- Footer includes all available contact methods.
